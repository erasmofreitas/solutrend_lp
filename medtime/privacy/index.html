MedTime by Solutrend

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 24, 2026 · Applies to the MedTime mobile app (Android and iOS)

In plain language

  • Your medications, schedules and dose history are stored only on your device. MedTime has no servers and no user accounts — we never see this data.
  • We show ads through Google AdMob, which may collect device and advertising identifiers to personalize them. You control this in-app: Settings → Manage ad consent.
  • We use Firebase Crashlytics to learn about crashes so we can fix them. Crash reports never include your medication data.
  • There's nothing to delete on a server if you stop using the app — uninstalling it removes your local data. You can also export a copy any time from Settings → Export data.

1.Who we are

MedTime is developed by Solutrend ("Solutrend", "we", "us"). MedTime is an offline-first medication reminder app: it helps you keep track of medications, schedules, and whether you took a dose. This policy explains what information the app and the services it relies on (advertising and crash reporting) handle, and the choices available to you.

This policy is written to meet the requirements of the EU/EEA General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Brazil's Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), regardless of where you're located.

2.Information we collect

Data that never leaves your device

MedTime has no backend server and does not require an account, email address, or sign-in of any kind. The following is stored exclusively in a local database on your device, using your operating system's app storage:

  • Medication names, dosages, and instructions you enter
  • Reminder schedules (times and days of the week)
  • Dose history (when you marked a medication as taken)
  • App preferences (language, notification settings, onboarding status)

We have no way to access, view, or transmit this information — it simply isn't sent anywhere. If you uninstall the app or clear its storage, this data is permanently deleted.

Data collected by advertising (Google AdMob)

MedTime is funded by ads served through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google's SDK may collect, on our behalf and its own:

Data typePurpose
Advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID / Apple IDFA)Ad personalization and frequency capping
Coarse device and network informationAd delivery and fraud prevention
Approximate location (derived from IP address)Regional ad relevance and legal consent requirements
Ad interaction events (impressions, clicks)Measuring ad performance

On iOS, this only happens if you grant permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. In the EEA, UK, and other regions where required, a consent form (via Google's User Messaging Platform) is shown before any personalized ad is requested — see Your choices and rights.

Data collected for crash reporting (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the app crashes or encounters an error, Firebase Crashlytics collects a crash report containing: device model and OS version, app version, and a stack trace of the error. We deliberately never include medication names, schedules, or any content you've entered in these reports.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or any government-issued ID. We do not request access to your contacts, photos, camera, or precise GPS location.

3.How we use information

  • Local data is used only to run the app's core features on your device: reminders, history, and the display of your medications.
  • Advertising data is used by Google to select and measure the ads shown in the app, which is how MedTime remains free.
  • Crash data is used to identify and fix bugs.

We do not sell your data in the traditional sense (there's no data broker relationship), but under CCPA's broad definition, sharing device identifiers with an ad network for personalized advertising can count as a "sale" or "share" — see your opt-out options below.

5.Your choices and rights

Ad consent

Open Settings → Manage ad consent in the app at any time to review or withdraw your advertising consent choices. This option is shown when required by your region.

If you're in the EEA, UK, or Brazil (GDPR / LGPD)

You have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your data, restrict or object to its processing, and withdraw consent at any time. Because your medication data lives only on your device, you exercise these rights directly:

  • Access / export: Settings → Export data copies everything as JSON.
  • Correction / deletion: edit or deactivate a medication directly in the app; uninstalling the app deletes everything.
  • Object to advertising processing: use the ad consent manager described above, or your device's "reset advertising ID" / "limit ad tracking" system setting.

For requests concerning data held by Google (AdMob/Crashlytics) as an independent controller, contact Google directly using the links in the table above.

If you're in California (CCPA/CPRA)

You have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. You can opt out of personalized advertising using the ad consent manager in Settings, or your device's ad tracking controls. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16.

6.Data retention

Local data is retained on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Advertising identifiers and crash reports are retained by Google/Firebase according to their own retention schedules, generally on the order of months, after which they are deleted or fully anonymized.

7.Security

Because your medication data never leaves your device, it is protected by your device's own security (screen lock, disk encryption). We recommend keeping your device's operating system up to date and using a screen lock, since anyone with unlocked access to your phone can open the app.

8.International data transfers

Your medication data is never transferred anywhere — it stays on your device. Advertising and crash-reporting data processed by Google may be transferred to and processed in the United States and other countries where Google operates, under the safeguards described in Google's privacy policy.

9.Children's privacy

MedTime is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required by your local law), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided data through the app's advertising or crash-reporting components, please contact us so we can address it.

10.Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective date" above, and, where required by law, we'll provide additional notice.
